Shopify — Financial Data Availability

Reference document for accounting & financial reconciliation  ·  May 2026

This document outlines what financial information Shopify makes available for reporting and reconciliation purposes. The data covers three levels: payouts (transfers to your bank), individual transactions that make up each payout, and order-level financial detail including taxes and discounts. It also lists what Shopify does not make available and where to find that information instead.

Important: Payout and transaction-level data is only available for stores using Shopify Payments as their payment processor. Stores using third-party processors (Stripe, PayPal, etc.) do not have access to this data through Shopify.

Level 1 — Payout Report

Per bank transfer

For every transfer Shopify sends to your bank account, the following information is available. The summary fields give you a categorised breakdown of what made up that payout — similar to what you would see in the Shopify Admin under Finances → Payouts.

Data Point Description
Payout amount (net)The total amount transferred to your bank — after all fees, refunds, and adjustments have been applied
Issue date & timeThe exact date and time the transfer was initiated
Transfer statusWhether the payout is: Scheduled, In Transit, Paid, Failed, or Cancelled
Transfer directionWhether funds are going into the bank (deposit) or back to Shopify (withdrawal, e.g. after a failed payout reversal)
Bank reference numberThe unique trace/reference number assigned by the financial institution — use this to match the transfer in your bank statement
Legal entityThe business entity associated with the payout
Charges — grossTotal revenue collected from customers during this payout period, before any fees
Charges — feesTotal Shopify Payments processing fees deducted from customer charges
Refunds — grossTotal gross amount refunded to customers
Refunds — fees returnedProcessing fees credited back to you for refunded transactions
Adjustments — grossGross amount of adjustments, including chargeback resolutions and dispute outcomes
Adjustments — feesFees associated with adjustments and chargebacks
Capital advance repayments — grossGross amount deducted for Shopify Capital loan repayments (if applicable)
Capital advance repayments — feesFees associated with Shopify Capital repayments
Reserved funds — grossAmount of funds held back by Shopify as a reserve (risk management)
Reserved funds — feesFees on any reserved fund balance
Re-attempted payouts — grossGross of transfers that were previously failed and are being re-sent

Level 2 — Individual Transaction Detail

Per transaction within a payout

Each payout is composed of many individual transactions. For every charge, refund, chargeback, or adjustment, the following information is available. This is the level where you can tie a specific bank payout line back to a customer order.

Data Point Description
Gross amountThe full amount of the transaction before any fees are deducted
Processing feeThe Shopify Payments fee deducted for this specific transaction
Net amountThe amount that actually changes your Shopify Payments balance (gross minus fee)
Transaction dateThe date and time this transaction was processed
Transaction categoryWhat type of money movement this is: Customer Payment, Refund, Dispute/Chargeback, Bank Transfer, Manual Adjustment, or System Correction
Associated orderThe customer order that caused this transaction — links the payout line back to a specific sale
Order transaction referenceThe specific payment event within that order (e.g. the capture, the refund)
Source reference IDShopify's internal ID for the underlying charge, refund, or dispute
Adjustment reasonA text explanation of why an adjustment was made (only present for manual adjustments)
Related adjustment ordersAny orders linked to a dispute or chargeback resolution
Payout this belongs toWhich bank transfer this transaction is included in
Test transaction flagWhether this was a test transaction (not real money)

Transaction Categories Explained

CategoryMeaning
Customer PaymentA successful charge on a customer's card — money coming into your balance
RefundMoney returned to a customer — reduces your balance
Dispute / ChargebackA customer has disputed a charge with their bank; Shopify holds the funds pending resolution
Bank TransferThe payout itself — the movement of your balance to your bank account
Manual AdjustmentA correction applied by Shopify (e.g. to correct an error or settle a dispute in your favour)
Adjustment ReversalA reversal of a previously applied manual adjustment
System CorrectionAn automatic balance correction applied by Shopify's systems

Level 3 — Order Financial Detail

Per customer order

For each order, Shopify provides a detailed financial breakdown including taxes collected per authority, discounts applied, shipping charged, and refund history. This level allows you to understand the full composition of what a customer paid and how it breaks down for tax reporting.

Data Point Description
Current total priceThe order's total today, including taxes and shipping, after any refunds or edits
Current subtotalProduct subtotal today, after refunds and edits, excluding taxes and shipping
Current total taxTax amount currently owed on this order, after refunds
Current total discountsTotal discounts currently applied to the order after refunds
Current shipping amountShipping amount currently charged after any shipping refunds
Current additional feesDuties, import fees, and other additional charges currently outstanding
Original total priceThe total price at the time the order was placed (before any edits or refunds)
Original subtotalProduct subtotal at the time of order
Original total taxTotal tax collected at the time of order creation
Original total discountsTotal discounts applied at the time of order creation
Original shipping amountShipping charged at the time of order creation
Tax lines — per authorityBreakdown of tax collected by each tax authority: authority name (e.g. "CA State Tax", "EU VAT"), tax rate (%), and exact amount collected
Tax authority liability flagIndicates whether Shopify is responsible for remitting this particular tax on your behalf
Per-product taxTax amount broken down per individual line item and per tax authority
Per-product discountsDiscount amounts applied to each individual product line, and the discount source (coupon, automatic rule, etc.)
Per-product original priceThe listed price of each product before any discounts
Per-product discounted priceThe final price of each product after all discounts
Payment statusCurrent payment status: Pending, Authorised, Partially Paid, Paid, Partially Refunded, Fully Refunded, or Voided
Payment transactionsEach individual payment event: amount, payment method, gateway, status, and authorisation code
Refund historyFull record of all refunds on the order: refunded products, refunded shipping, dates, and amounts
Connecting orders to payouts: Each transaction in Level 2 includes a reference to the customer order it came from. This allows you to trace any payout line back to the original sale and see the full tax and discount breakdown for that order.

What Shopify Does Not Make Available

Gaps & Limitations
The following are data points commonly needed for complete financial reporting that Shopify does not expose. Each entry includes where to find this information instead.
Missing Data Point Explanation & Where to Find It
Shopify plan / subscription cost The monthly or annual Shopify subscription fee is not included in any report. It is charged separately on a Shopify billing invoice — find it in the Shopify Admin under Settings → Billing, or on your Shopify billing email.
Processing fee rate per card type Shopify shows the fee amount per transaction but does not disclose the breakdown by card brand (e.g. Visa vs Amex vs international). The rate tiers depend on your Shopify plan and are shown in Admin under Settings → Payments.
Third-party app subscription costs Costs for Shopify apps (shipping tools, marketing platforms, etc.) are not included in payout or financial reports. They appear on your separate Shopify billing invoice alongside your plan cost.
Tax remitted by Shopify on your behalf In certain jurisdictions (e.g. US marketplace facilitator states, Managed Markets), Shopify collects and remits sales tax directly to the tax authority without passing it through your payout at all. The tax amount simply does not appear — you receive only the net sale price. Shopify provides a separate tax remittance summary in the Admin under Analytics → Reports → Taxes.
Currency conversion (FX) rate applied When orders are placed in a different currency to your payout currency, Shopify converts at an internal rate. Both the customer's currency amount and your currency amount are available, but the exact exchange rate used is not disclosed. You can infer it by dividing one by the other.
Shopify Shipping label costs Postage purchased through Shopify Shipping is deducted from your Shopify Payments balance as a system adjustment. While the deduction appears in your transactions, it does not carry a label reference or tracking number — making line-by-line reconciliation against shipments difficult. Use the Shopify Admin shipping reports for detail.
Shopify Email & marketing add-on costs Usage charges for Shopify Email and other built-in marketing tools are billed on your Shopify invoice, not deducted from your payout balance. Not available in financial reports — check the billing invoice.
Shopify Capital — advance terms & balance The gross amount of Capital repayments deducted from each payout is visible, but the original advance amount, total amount owed, repayment percentage rate, and remaining balance are not available in reports. Shopify provides a separate Capital statement in Admin under Capital.
Chargeback / dispute evidence & final rulings Dispute records show the current status (open, won, lost) and amount, but the full dispute timeline, evidence submitted, and final ruling communication are not available in data exports. They can only be viewed in the Shopify Admin under Finances → Disputes.
Orders older than 60 days (default limit) By default, Shopify only returns the last 60 days of order history. Full historical access must be explicitly enabled. If your reports are missing older orders, this restriction is the likely cause.
Tax filing / remittance reports The tax data available on individual orders shows what was collected from customers — not what has been remitted to tax authorities. Shopify does not produce a tax-filing-ready report through its data. Use the Tax report exports in Admin under Analytics → Reports → Taxes, or pass the order-level tax data to your accountant or tax software for filing.

Key Points for Accounting & Reconciliation

Processing fee = Shopify's cut per transaction.
For every customer payment, Shopify deducts a processing fee (e.g. 2.5% + a fixed amount per transaction depending on your plan). The difference between the gross charge amount and the fee is what enters your Shopify Payments balance — and the sum of all these net amounts equals the payout you receive.
Tax on orders is not tax on your fees.
The tax figures in Level 3 are taxes the customer paid on their purchase — money you collected on behalf of tax authorities. These are entirely separate from any VAT/GST that Shopify may charge you on its own subscription and processing fees, which appears only on the Shopify billing invoice.
Two currency amounts on every order.
Every monetary value is provided in both your store's accounting currency and the currency the customer actually paid in. Always use the store currency column for your own bookkeeping and financial statements.
One order can create multiple payout lines.
A single customer order may appear more than once in your transaction history — for example, once when payment was captured and again if a partial refund was later issued. All are linked to the same order reference.
